## Image Specifications

### Hero Images:
- **Size**: 1200x1600px (3:4 aspect ratio)
- **Format**: JPG or WebP
- **Style**: Premium, editorial, calm, professional

### Infographics:
- **Size**: 1920x1080px (16:9) or 1200x1200px (1:1)
- **Format**: JPG or WebP
- **Style**: Clean, technical, modern, educational

## Quick Generation Workflow

1. **Choose your AI tool** (Midjourney recommended for premium quality)
2. **Copy prompts** from above
3. **Generate images** one by one
4. **Download and rename** files to match the required names
5. **Place in `/public/img/`** directory
6. **Test the page** - images will load automatically!
