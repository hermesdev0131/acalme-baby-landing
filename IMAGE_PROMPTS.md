## 🎨 **GENERAL STYLE GUIDELINES**

### Color Palette:
- **Primary:** Warm creams, beiges, soft whites
- **Accent:** Soft teal/aqua (trust color)
- **Tone:** Calm, premium, maternal warmth
- **Avoid:** Harsh colors, cold tones, overly bright images

### Photography Style:
- **Quality:** Premium, editorial, professional
- **Lighting:** Natural, soft, warm
- **Composition:** Clean, minimal, focused
- **Mood:** Calm, peaceful, reassuring, premium

### Technical Requirements:
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1920px width (or appropriate for aspect ratio)
- **Format:** JPG or WebP (optimize for web)
- **File Size:** Optimize for web (aim for <500KB per image)
- **Aspect Ratios:** Maintain specified ratios for each image

## 💡 **TIPS FOR AI IMAGE GENERATION**

1. **Use negative prompts** to avoid unwanted elements:
   - "no harsh lighting, no cold colors, no cluttered background, no medical equipment visible, no text overlays"

2. **Iterate on prompts** - adjust based on results:
   - If too medical → add "approachable, friendly"
   - If too cold → add "warm tones, soft lighting"
   - If too cluttered → add "minimal, clean background"

3. **Style references:**
   - "Similar to Cradlewise photography"
   - "Premium baby brand aesthetic"
   - "Editorial lifestyle photography"

4. **For medical illustrations:**
   - Balance accuracy with approachability
   - Use soft colors, not clinical
   - Keep labels minimal and clear
